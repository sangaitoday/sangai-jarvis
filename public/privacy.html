Privacy Policy

Effective date: 29 September 2026

This policy explains how this browser-only SangAI voice assistant handles information. You can reach us at sang@sangai.today.

On-device models and voices

When you pick a model that runs in your browser, the model files are downloaded to your device and the conversation is processed there. Those messages are not sent to any chat server. On-device voices work the same way: their model files are downloaded from this site or from Hugging Face (huggingface.co) the first time you use them, and speech is synthesized in your browser. Hugging Face sees the download request, like any website you visit, but never the text being spoken.

Voice

Voice input uses your browser's built-in speech recognition. Depending on your browser, audio may be processed on your device or sent to the browser maker's speech service (for example Google for Chrome) under that company's privacy policy. Spoken replies are generated in your browser using either your browser's speech synthesis or downloaded ONNX voice models.

Information stored in your browser

The app saves your settings, such as conversation mode, model and voice, in your browser's local storage. Downloaded model and voice files are kept in your browser's cache and origin-private storage so they don't need to be downloaded again. This data stays on your device, and you can remove it at any time from the Model & Voices panel or by clearing site data in your browser.
